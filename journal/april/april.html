4/6 - Woke up, packed, translated convo with our driver (impressed with the cars in thailand) - Lounge at bangkok, even the satellite terminal is nicer than most US airports. Awesome last thai meals for lunch - Flight Bangkok > Da Nang 4/5 - Woke up, worked out at the hotel, talked to Frank the old forest service guy from Nevada - Took the skytrain to a café called Mocha and Muffins in the boujee part of Bangkok, continued realizing how insanely expansive the city is. Thailand can be cheap if you want it to be, but expensive if you let it - Grab to where the bike tour started, just our guide Mo and a guy from Australia waiting for us - Absolutely amazing Bike tour winding through the Thuinong(?) neighborhood, through back alleys, stopping a few times at a temple, a small restaurant for some type of angel hair orange curry, a pineapple stand, etc. We got to a river market where we walked around, then sat inside an old couple's house for lunch/dinner which was amazing (holy basil pork, rice, panang curry w/tofu, mango sticky rice, etc.). The old guy was a former cop and for the last few years a company came to them and proposed turning their living room into a restaurant. Wonder how common that is. We bike back in the dark through even tighter and cooler alleyways, honestly one of my favorite tours ever. - We walk across the big bridge nearby to the flower market, which we pass through until we Grab over to the chinatown area for drinks. We try and fail at a couple of bars that either didn't take card or were strangely expensive before winding up at Brown Sugar jazz bar, and awesome experience where a bunch of talented local musicians jammed mere feet away from us. Pretty meh drinks but whatever. Absolutely wiped from the day, we end up grabbing back to our area, doing a snack run at 7/11, and finally crashing at the hotel while looking for lodging for the next week or so in Da Nang (classic procrastination)
